Shoal AI

Predicts which water groups are likely to escalate. It is an expert-weighted score with a recency-weighted trend forecast, not yet trained on confirmed outcomes. Treat it as screening support, not a diagnosis.

Laboratory confirmation

A laboratory result upgrades certainty to Observed and raises the alert level for confirmed infectious causes.

Take 3 to 5 fresh sick or just-dead fish per farm, bag each farm separately, keep on ice at 2 to 4 °C and deliver within 24 hours.

Detection settings

Each water group is tested against its own recent baseline (EARS C2 style), a cumulative-sum chart (CUSUM) tracks sustained excess, and neighbouring groups inherit risk from groups in Warning.

One day, Ghana predicts fish disease outbreaks before they spread

The problem

Farms on the same river, lake or reservoir share disease. Deaths are usually recorded farm by farm, if at all, so one outbreak can reach many farms before anyone is warned.

What ShoalWatch does

  1. Farmers report fish deaths from a phone.
  2. Reports are grouped by shared water body.
  3. Each water body is compared with its own baseline, and neighbours inherit risk.
  4. Shoal AI forecasts escalation, laboratory results confirm the cause, and farmers are warned by SMS or WhatsApp.

Where it stands

Working prototype. The demo farms are simulated. Shoal AI uses expert-set weights and has not yet been trained on confirmed outbreaks.

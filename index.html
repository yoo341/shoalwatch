Aquatic health early warning

Active alerts

Report suspected mortality

Count fish lost to suspected disease. Low oxygen and poor water quality also kill fish, so record them under cause.

Laboratory confirmation

A laboratory result upgrades certainty to Observed and raises the alert level for confirmed infectious causes.

Take 3 to 5 fresh sick or just-dead fish per farm, bag each farm separately, keep on ice at 2 to 4 °C and deliver within 24 hours.

Detection settings

Each water group is tested against its own recent baseline (EARS C2 style), a cumulative-sum chart (CUSUM) tracks sustained excess, and neighbouring groups inherit risk from groups in Warning.
